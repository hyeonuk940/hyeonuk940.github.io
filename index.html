Projects

Early ICU-to-Ward Transfer Prediction in Sepsis

A LightGBM model, developed on Sepsis-3 patients in MIMIC-IV and externally validated on eICU-CRD, that predicts which ICU patients can be safely transferred to the ward 24–72 hours after ICU admission without readmission or death within 7 days. The repository includes the cohort-extraction SQL, the full analysis pipeline (nested CV, SHAP, calibration, decision-curve and hospital-level analyses) and the aggregate results reported in the paper.
